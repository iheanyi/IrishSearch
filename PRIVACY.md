# IrishSearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

IrishSearch is an independent Android app for browsing the University of Notre Dame's public class listings and planning schedules. It does not require an account. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Information sent when you browse

When you load semesters, search classes, or open section details, the app sends requests directly from your device to Notre Dame's public PATH class search (`classsearch.nd.edu`). A request can include the selected semester, subject, search words, course code, and section number. As with any website request, the university may receive your IP address and technical request details. Notre Dame controls its own service and privacy practices.

IrishSearch does not send these requests through a developer-operated server. It does not receive your university credentials or access private student records.

## Information stored on your device

Saved classes, schedule names and contents, and seat-alert choices are stored on your device. IrishSearch does not upload this information to a developer-operated server. Android backup is disabled for the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

If you turn on seat alerts, Android periodically runs a constrained background check for watched sections against PATH. Checks require a network connection and a battery that is not low. You can turn alerts off for any section. Android's notification permission is requested only when you turn alerts on. Delivery time depends on Android scheduling and current PATH availability.

## Bookstore links

If you choose to check textbooks and materials, IrishSearch opens the Notre Dame bookstore in your browser. The bookstore and browser then handle that visit under their own policies. IrishSearch does not receive your bookstore activity.

## Contact and changes

For questions about this app or this policy, open an issue in the [IrishSearch GitHub repository](https://github.com/iheanyi/IrishSearch). Changes to this policy will be published in the repository and reflected in future app updates.
