# IrishSearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

IrishSearch is an independent Android app developed by Iheanyi Ekechukwu for browsing the University of Notre Dame's public class listings and planning schedules. It does not require an account. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Information sent when you browse

When you load semesters, search classes, or open section details, the app sends requests over HTTPS directly from your device to Notre Dame's public PATH class search (`classsearch.nd.edu`). A request can include the selected semester, subject, search words, filters, instructor query, course code, and section number. As with any website request, the university receives your IP address and technical request details. Notre Dame controls its own service and privacy practices. IrishSearch does not control the university's server logs, retention periods, or deletion processes and does not claim that these requests are never retained.

IrishSearch does not send these requests through a developer-operated server. It does not receive your university credentials or access private student records.

## Information stored on your device

Saved classes, schedule names and contents, personal commitments, latest seat-check status, and seat-alert choices are stored in the app's private storage on your device. IrishSearch does not upload this information to a developer-operated server. Android backup is disabled for the app. You can remove saved items in the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it removes all its local data.

If you turn on seat alerts, Android periodically runs a constrained background check for watched sections against PATH. Checks require a network connection and a battery that is not low. You can turn alerts off for any section. Android's notification permission is requested only when you turn alerts on. Delivery time depends on Android scheduling and current PATH availability.

## Files you export or restore

You can save a backup of your library, export a calendar, or share a schedule image. These files can include course choices, schedule names, and personal commitments. Android asks you to choose the destination or receiving app. A cloud storage provider or receiving app handles the exported file under its own policies; IrishSearch does not upload it to a developer-operated server. Exported files remain at their chosen destination after the app is uninstalled. Calendar exports are snapshots, not live calendar synchronization.

Restoring a backup reads only the file you select and adds missing records to your device. It keeps existing records and leaves alerts off for newly imported classes. You can remove exported files using the storage provider or app where you saved them.

## Bookstore links

If you choose to check textbooks and materials, IrishSearch opens the Notre Dame bookstore in your browser. The bookstore and browser then handle that visit under their own policies. IrishSearch does not receive your bookstore activity.

## Contact and changes

For questions about this app or this policy, open an issue in the [IrishSearch GitHub repository](https://github.com/iheanyi/IrishSearch/issues). Issues are public, so do not include private information. Changes to this policy will be published in the repository and reflected in future app updates.
